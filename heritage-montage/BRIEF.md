# Bihar Heritage Reel

## Format

- Duration: 14.56 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Treatment: restrained editorial travel montage
- Audio: deliberate silence; the homepage video is muted
- Palette: warm cream, deep teal, muted terracotta, desaturated heritage photography
- Motion: slow alternating Ken Burns pushes with soft crossfades

## Concept

A quiet visual passage through Bihar: six places, each held long enough to notice. The reel should feel like a page turning slowly rather than a fast tourism montage.

## Delivery

The website consumes `client/src/assets/heritage/heritage-reel.mp4` and `client/src/assets/heritage/heritage-reel-poster.jpg`. The build source is `build_heritage_reel.py`, which can be rerun after replacing or reordering the optimized stills in `heritage-assets/optimized/`.

## Asset provenance

The stills are documented in `heritage-assets/manifest.json`. They were sourced from Wikimedia Commons and optimized locally for web/video use. The homepage includes a visible image-credit disclosure linking back to the source pages and licenses.
