# Bihar Heritage Reel Storyboard

## Scene 1 — Bodh Gaya

**Role:** Establish. The Mahabodhi Temple fills the frame with a gentle forward push. The opening holds on warm stone and morning light, introducing the idea of a slower route.

**Transition:** Soft crossfade into the red-brick geometry of Nalanda.

## Scene 2 — Nalanda

**Role:** Develop. The monastery ruins drift slowly from a slightly wider crop toward the courtyard, letting the long horizontal lines create a meditative rhythm.

**Transition:** Soft crossfade, preserving the warm terracotta register.

## Scene 3 — Rajgir

**Role:** Develop. The historic Jarasandh site and surrounding hills receive a slow outward movement, giving the viewer room to see the landscape around the structure.

**Transition:** Soft crossfade into the stronger architectural silhouette of Golghar.

## Scene 4 — Golghar

**Role:** Turn. The centered historic form holds for a quiet beat while the camera-like push brings its curved profile forward.

**Transition:** Soft crossfade into the lower, wider view of Kesariya.

## Scene 5 — Vikramshila

**Role:** Breathe. The panoramic ruins act as a visual pause, with a slow lateral drift across the low horizon.

**Transition:** Soft crossfade into the final stupa scene.

## Scene 6 — Kesariya

**Role:** Resolve. The stupa’s broad silhouette receives a restrained push-in and holds at the end, leaving the viewer with an open horizon rather than a hard sales beat.

**Transition:** Hold on the final frame; the website continues into the planner content.

## Rhythm

`slow hold → slow drift → gentle widen → architectural push → panoramic breathe → final hold`

The reel has no voiceover or music so it can sit quietly inside the homepage. The website supplies the interaction through scroll position, muted autoplay, and an accessible play/pause control.
